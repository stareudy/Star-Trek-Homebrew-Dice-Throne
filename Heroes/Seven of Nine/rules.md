## BRAINSTORMING

These notes were recovered from a previous ChatGPT conversation summary.
They are design concepts rather than finalized rules. The current timing
decision is that Borg Efficiency applies only to the Offensive Roll Phase.
Ability and card slots above remain unassigned placeholders.

### Borg Efficiency
Proposed positive status effect with a stack limit of 2.
Spend 1 token at the beginning of your Offensive Roll Phase to gain either
  an additional die or an additional roll attempt.
Spend 2 tokens to gain both benefits.
Tokens should be relatively difficult to generate: powerful but scarce.
Possible generation requirement: a difficult dice combination, such as
  4 or 5 sixes. This requirement is not finalized.
Seven would need additional physical dice beyond the standard 5.

### Adaptation
Inspired by the Borg's ability to adapt to enemy attacks.
Seven could gain an Adaptation token after suffering certain negative
  effects, eventually allowing her to resist or prevent those effects.
Rules are not finalized; this remains a brainstorming concept rather
  than an additional leaflet token entry.

### Nanoprobes / Borg Nanites
Earlier notes called this concept Borg Nanites; the leaflet token is
  currently named Nanoprobes.
Seven could use these beneficially, potentially healing or removing
  negative status effects.
The planned Borg Queen boss could use nanoprobes offensively,
  accumulating toward temporary Assimilation.
Seven and the Doctor could accelerate the removal of Assimilation in
  a future Dice Throne Adventures campaign.
Rules are not finalized.

### Other Ability Concepts
Tricorder Scan: Expose vulnerabilities or generate Borg Efficiency.
Resistance Is Futile: Powerful offensive attack.
Regeneration Cycle: Healing and status-effect removal.
Astrometrics: Card draw or deck manipulation.
Collective Knowledge: Generate cards or CP.
Humanity Is Irrelevant: Trade defense for offensive power.
You Will Comply: Force opponent dice manipulation.
These concepts have not been assigned to ability or card slots.
